Helium calculates a GPA for each Class Group once it has a grade scale and a class with credits has a grade. It shows in the **Overall Grade** widget on the [**Grades**](https://app.heliumedu.com/grades) page. To choose your school's scale, see [Choosing a Grade Scale](/support/grades-and-progress/choosing-a-grade-scale/).

## Adding Credits

Credits decide how much each class counts toward your GPA. A class with no credits isn't counted.

  1. Open [**Classes**](https://app.heliumedu.com/classes) and click the class
  2. On the **Details** tab, set **Credits**
  3. Click **Save**

## How GPA Is Calculated

  * Each class earns the grade point of the band its grade falls in, shown with the band's letter next to the class on the **Grades** page (e.g., "B+ &bull; 3.50 GP"). A class without credits shows only the letter
  * Your GPA is each class's grade point times its credits, added up, over the total credits

## GPA Across All Terms

The **Overall Grade** widget also shows **All**, your GPA across every group on the same grade scale as your most recent group that has one, weighted by credits, plus any prior credits. The example schedule isn't included. **All** shows once more than one group counts toward it, or once you add prior credits.

### Adding Prior Credits

If you started using Helium partway through your degree, add the credits and GPA from your transcript to count them toward **All**.

  1. Open [**Settings → Preferences**](https://app.heliumedu.com/settings/preferences)
  2. Set **Prior credits** and **Prior GPA**
  3. Click **Save**

Prior credits count toward **All** at your prior GPA, like one more term on its grade scale. They don't change any term's GPA, and they don't count until a group has a grade scale. Until a class with credits is graded, **All** shows your prior GPA.
