A grade scale turns each class's percentage into a letter grade, like 93% and up earning an A, and into a grade point, like that A earning a 4.0. You get letter grades as soon as you choose a scale. Add credits to your classes too, and Helium calculates your GPA; see [Tracking Your GPA](/support/grades-and-progress/tracking-your-gpa/).

## Choosing a Scale for a Term

Each Class Group has its own scale. A new group uses the scale of your most recent group, or **Percentages only** for your first.

  1. Open [**Classes**](https://app.heliumedu.com/classes)
  2. Open the group menu at the top and click the edit (pencil) icon next to the group
  3. Choose a **Grade scale**
  4. Click **Save**

Each scale in the list shows its letter range, like "A+ to F". Pick the one that matches the grading scale in your syllabus or school handbook, then click the table icon next to the list to check every letter, cutoff, and grade point against it.

**Percentages only** keeps your grades as percentages, with no letters or GPA.

A scale set through the [Helium API](/support/sync-and-integration/using-the-helium-api/) or an import that doesn't match a preset shows as **Custom**.

## Letter Grades Without GPA

If your school uses letter grades but you don't track GPA, choose a scale and leave your classes' **Credits** empty. Each class shows its letter on the [**Grades**](https://app.heliumedu.com/grades) page, and Helium doesn't calculate a GPA.

## A Class With Its Own Scale

If a syllabus grades differently from the rest of your term, like whole letters in a group graded with +/-, give that class its own scale. The class's **Grade scale** only shows when its group has a scale.

  1. Open [**Classes**](https://app.heliumedu.com/classes) and click the class
  2. On the **Details** tab, choose a **Grade scale**
  3. Click **Save**

The list only offers scales whose grades your group's scale also uses, and it's unavailable when nothing else fits. **Same as group** uses the group's scale. If you change the group's scale, a class scale that no longer fits goes back to **Same as group**.

When a class has a grade scale, its card on the **Classes** page shows it.
