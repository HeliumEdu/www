## How to Open the Grade Calculator

  1. Open [**Grades**](https://app.heliumedu.com/grades)
  2. Click the **Grade Calculator** icon (calculator), to the right of the "Grade Trend" header
  3. If you have multiple classes, a list will appear — select the class you want to calculate for

---

## What Could I Get?

Projects your overall grade by letting you set hypothetical scores for every ungraded assignment.

Each ungraded assignment appears as a row with a slider and a percentage. Drag the slider to set a hypothetical score — your projected grade updates at the bottom of the dialog.

### Default Slider Positions

Each slider starts at your current category average. If a category has no graded work yet, sliders in that category default to 100%.

### Projected Grade Indicator

The projected grade at the bottom is color-coded against your at-risk threshold:

  * **Green** — projected grade is at or above your at-risk threshold
  * **Yellow** — projected grade is below your at-risk threshold

### Reset

Click **Reset** to restore all sliders to their category-average defaults.

### How the Projection Works

For weighted courses, each category's hypothetical grade averages your existing graded points with the hypothetical scores, then applies the category's weight. For non-weighted courses, raw points are summed across all assignments. Extra-credit categories (zero weight) are excluded.

---

## What Do I Need?

Calculates the exact score you need on a single remaining assignment in a category to achieve your desired overall grade.

### Requirements

This tab unlocks when a category has **exactly one assignment left to grade**. If no categories meet this requirement, the tab shows a "Come back later" message. Extra-credit categories (zero weight) are excluded.

### Steps

The dialog shows your current overall grade for the class at the top.

  1. Select the **target category** (the one containing the single remaining ungraded item)
  2. Enter your **desired overall grade**
  3. Click **Calculate**

The result will tell you one of the following:

  * **You need X%** on that category to reach your target
  * **You're already above your target** — your current grade already meets your target
  * **Score is unachievable** — the needed score exceeds 100%; adjust your target grade
