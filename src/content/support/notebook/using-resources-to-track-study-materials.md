The [**Resources**](https://app.heliumedu.com/resources) screen lets you track textbooks, software, websites, and any other study materials associated with your classes. Resources are organized into **Resource Groups** — for example, you could group them by term, category, etc.

Use the group dropdown at the top of the screen to switch between Resource Groups, or select **Show All** to view all resources at once.

## Creating a Resource Group

  1. Open [**Resources**](https://app.heliumedu.com/resources)
  2. Click the **+** button to create a new group
  3. Enter a **Title** for the group (e.g., "Spring 2026 Materials", "Textbooks", "Links")
  4. Optionally check **Hide this group's resources from the Planner** if you don't want them displayed on your Planner or offered in the Notebook's link picker
  5. Click **Save**

## Adding a Resource

  1. Select a Resource Group from the list, or select **Show All**
  2. Click the **+** button to add a resource
  3. Fill in the resource details:
     * **Group** — the Resource Group the resource belongs to (required). Defaults to the group you're viewing, or pick one if you're on **Show All**. Change it to move the resource to another group.
     * **Title** — the name of the resource (required)
     * **Classes** — classes this resource is associated with
     * **Website** — a URL for the resource (e.g., a publisher page, textbook, or link to an online app or tool)
  4. Click **Save**. The **Attachments** tab opens next, where you can add files.

## Attaching Files to a Resource

You can attach files to a resource, such as a PDF of a reading. Open the resource, go to the **Attachments** tab, click **Choose Files**, then **Upload**.

To download a resource's attachments without opening it, click the paperclip on its card. They also show on the assignments that use the resource.

## Deleting a Resource or Resource Group

Deleting a resource also deletes its note and attachments. Deleting a Resource Group deletes every resource in it, along with their notes and attachments.

## Resources and Hidden Class Groups

Classes in a hidden Class Group don't show on a resource's card or in its **Classes** field. The link isn't removed, and it shows again when you un-hide the group. See [Setting Up and Managing Classes](/support/classes/setting-up-and-managing-classes/).
