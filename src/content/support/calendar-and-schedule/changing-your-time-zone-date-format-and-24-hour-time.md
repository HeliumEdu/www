Helium sets your time zone, formats, and first day of the week from your device when you sign up. Change them in [**Settings → Preferences**](https://app.heliumedu.com/settings/preferences), then click **Save**. Your preferences apply on all your devices.

## Time Zone

Helium shows due times, events, and reminders in your **Time zone**. If times look an hour or more off, check this setting first.

When you change it, assignments and events keep the same moment, so their times shift to the new time zone. An assignment due at 11:59 PM Chicago time shows as 12:59 AM New York time. All-day items stay on the same date, and class schedules stay as entered.

## 24-Hour Time, Date Format, and Number Format

Under **General**, click the example you want:

  * **Time format** — 12-hour (like 2:30 PM) or 24-hour (like 14:30)
  * **Date format** — month first (like Nov 4, 2026) or day first (like 4 Nov 2026)
  * **Number format** — **1,234.5** or **1.234,5**

## First Day of the Week

Under **Calendar**, choose a day from **Week starts on**, such as **Monday**.
