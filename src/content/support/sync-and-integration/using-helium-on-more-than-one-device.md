Helium saves everything to your account, so your planner, notes, and grades are the same in the iOS app, the Android app, and on the web.

## Syncing

Changes save to your account when you save them, and notes save as you type. Your other devices pick up the changes the next time you open Helium on them, or when you come back after being away for a while. An open note updates with changes from your other devices when it has no unsaved changes of its own.

## Editing on Two Devices

If something changed on another device after you opened it, Helium asks before saving and shows when the other change was made:

  * **Save Changes** — saves your version over the other change
  * **Discard Changes** — shows the latest version instead of yours
  * **Keep Editing** — for notes, closes the message so you can review first

## Offline Notes and Unsaved Changes

Keep writing if you lose your connection. While Helium waits to save, the cloud icon in the note editor shows a line through it. Helium keeps trying, or click the icon to try again right away.

If Helium closes before a note saves, your changes stay on that device:

  * Open the note again, and Helium recovers and saves your changes
  * A new note that hadn't saved yet comes back the next time you open the Notebook
  * If the note changed on another device in the meantime, click **Recover** to keep your changes or **Discard** to keep the other version

Signing out of that device removes them.
