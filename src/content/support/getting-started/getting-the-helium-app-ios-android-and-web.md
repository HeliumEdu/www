Helium is available as a native app for iOS and Android, and also runs in any modern web browser. Your data syncs automatically across all platforms. See [Using Helium on More Than One Device](/support/sync-and-integration/using-helium-on-more-than-one-device/).

## iOS (iPhone and iPad)

  1. Open the **[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/helium-student-planner/id6758323154)** on your iPhone or iPad
  2. Search for **Helium - Student Planner**
  3. Click **Get** to install
  4. Open the app and sign in to your existing account, or create a new one

## Android (Phone and Tablet)

  1. Open the **[Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.heliumedu.heliumapp)** on your Android device
  2. Search for **Helium - Student Planner**
  3. Click **Install**
  4. Open the app and sign in to your existing account, or create a new one

## Web

No installation is required to use Helium in a browser. Visit [**app.heliumedu.com**](https://app.heliumedu.com) and sign in from any device.

## Home Screen Widgets

The **Agenda** widget shows what's due without opening the app: overdue assignments, everything due today, and what's coming up over the next 7 days. Check an assignment's box to mark it complete, or tap an assignment to open it in Helium.

> **Note:** Changes made on the web or another device won't appear until you reopen Helium on the device with the widget, so it can refresh.

### iOS

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle
  2. Tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget** (on iOS 17, tap **+**)
  3. Search for **Helium**, choose **Agenda** and a size, then tap **Add Widget**

To add it to your Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap **Customize**, choose your **Lock Screen**, and tap the widget area to add **Agenda**.

### Android

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your home screen, then tap **Widgets**
  2. Find **Helium**, then touch and hold **Agenda** and drag it onto your home screen
  3. To resize it, touch and hold the widget, then drag its edges

## Signing In on a New Device

When you sign in on a new device, all your classes, assignments, grades, and settings load automatically. There is no manual sync step required.
