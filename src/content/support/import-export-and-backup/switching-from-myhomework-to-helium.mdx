myHomework doesn't offer a one-click data export, but you can still get your schedule out — as a calendar feed, or by hand — before bringing it into Helium, a free, open-source student planner for iOS, Android, and the web.

## Export Your myHomework Data

myHomework can publish your schedule as a calendar feed.

  1. In myHomework, open **Calendar Settings** and enable **external calendar access**.
  2. Copy the calendar (`.ics`) URL it gives you.
  3. Add that URL to a calendar you control — in Google Calendar, use **Add calendar → From URL**; in Apple Calendar, use **File → New Calendar Subscription**.
  4. Export that calendar to an **`.ics` file** from Google or Apple Calendar and save it somewhere you'll find it again — you'll hand this file to the AI assistant in the next step.

If your account doesn't have this option, jot down (or screenshot) your classes, their meeting times, and your upcoming assignments instead — you'll enter those directly in the next step.

A calendar export carries your **class schedule and assignment due dates**. Grades and category weights don't travel in a calendar file — set those up in Helium as the term goes on.
