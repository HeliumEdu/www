iStudiez Pro is no longer being actively maintained. Its calendar sync still works today, but once iStudiez's cloud servers go offline, there's no way to pull your schedule back out.

## Export Your iStudiez Pro Data Before It Shuts Down

iStudiez Pro publishes your schedule as a calendar feed that lives on **iStudiez's own cloud servers**.

  1. In iStudiez Pro, open **Settings / Preferences → Sync** and sign in to your iStudiez Cloud account (if you aren't already).
  2. Open **Settings / Preferences → Calendars**, enable the calendar option, then open **Advanced** and copy the **Calendar URL**.
  3. Add that URL to a calendar you control — in Google Calendar, use **Add calendar → From URL**; in Apple Calendar, use **File → New Calendar Subscription**.
  4. Export that calendar to an **`.ics` file** from Google or Apple Calendar and save it somewhere you'll find it again — you'll hand this file to the AI assistant in the next step.

> **Important:** The Calendar URL only works while iStudiez's cloud is running. Export it now — once iStudiez shuts down, there's no way to retrieve it.

A calendar export carries your **class schedule and assignment due dates**. Grades, GPA, and credit hours don't travel in a calendar file — re-enter those in Helium as the term goes on.

## Bring Your Schedule into Helium

The fastest way to get an existing schedule into Helium is the **AI-assisted import**. You hand your exported iStudiez data to any modern AI assistant, it produces a Helium import file, and you upload that file to Helium.

  1. Follow the [**AI-assisted syllabus import**](/support/getting-started/where-to-start-with-helium/#import-a-whole-syllabus-with-ai) steps in *Where to Start with Helium*.
  2. When the assistant asks for your source material, attach the **`.ics` file** you saved above (or simply paste your list of classes, meeting times, and assignments).
  3. Answer its follow-up questions, download the import file it produces, and import it under [**Settings → Import / Export**](https://app.heliumedu.com/settings/import-export).

Prefer to set things up by hand? The [**manual walkthrough**](/support/getting-started/where-to-start-with-helium/#set-up-your-first-term) in the same article gets you to a usable term step by step. See [Importing & Exporting Your Data](/support/import-export-and-backup/importing-and-exporting-your-data/) for how Helium's import works.

If your school runs a rotating or A/B schedule, Helium supports that natively — see [Setting Up and Managing Classes](/support/classes/setting-up-and-managing-classes/).

> **Note:** To see what Helium does, [check out our homepage](https://www.heliumedu.com/#features).
