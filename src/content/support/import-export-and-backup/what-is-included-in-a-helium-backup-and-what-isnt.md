## Overview

Helium backups contain nearly all of your account data, exported in JSON format.

## What's Included

  * **Class Groups** — with classes, categories, schedules, assignments, and grades
  * **Events**
  * **Notes** — with their content, formatting, and related associations
  * **Reminders**
  * **External Calendars**
  * **Resource Groups** — with resources and their class associations

## What's Not Included

  * **Attachments** — uploaded files are not included
  * **Feed URLs** — these are regenerated per-account
  * **User Preferences**
  * **Login and Account Information**

> **Note:** To keep a copy of a note you can read outside Helium, save it as a PDF. See [Printing & Exporting to PDF or CSV](/support/import-export-and-backup/printing-and-exporting-to-pdf-or-csv/).
